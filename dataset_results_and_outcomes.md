## 3. Behavioral Drivers & Barriers (Multi-Select Breakdown)

### Top Payment Occasions ($N=111$)
1. **Bills / Recharge:** $63$ mentions ($56.76\%$)
2. **Shopping (Offline Retail / Malls):** $63$ mentions ($56.76\%$)
3. **Food / Cafés / Canteens:** $61$ mentions ($54.95\%$)
4. **Daily Groceries & Kirana:** $50$ mentions ($45.05\%$)
5. **Friends / Family (P2P Transfers):** $46$ mentions ($41.44\%$)
6. **Travel / Auto / Cab Commute:** $42$ mentions ($37.84\%$)
7. **Online E-Commerce Purchases:** $38$ mentions ($34.23\%$)
8. **College / Education Fees:** $27$ mentions ($24.32\%$)
9. **Subscriptions (OTT / Digital):** $16$ mentions ($14.41\%$)
10. **House Rent:** $15$ mentions ($13.51\%$)

### Why Users Pick Their Primary App ($N=111$)
1. **Speed & Ease of Checkout:** $50$ mentions ($45.05\%$)
2. **Habitual Default / Muscle Memory:** $36$ mentions ($32.43\%$)
3. **Peer Network ("Friends/family use it"):** $28$ mentions ($25.23\%$)
4. **Trust:** $19$ mentions ($17.12\%$)
5. **Features & Interface Experience:** $19$ mentions ($17.12\%$)
6. **Perceived Reliability:** $18$ mentions ($16.22\%$)
7. **Offers & Rewards:** $16$ mentions ($14.41\%$)
8. **Merchant QR Acceptance:** $13$ mentions ($11.71\%$)

### Why Users Do Not Use Paytm More (Paytm Barriers)
1. **Automatic Rival Default (Inertia):** $41$ mentions ($36.94\%$)
2. **Another App Feels Faster / Easier:** $27$ mentions ($24.32\%$)
3. **Friends / Family Are on Another App:** $17$ mentions ($15.32\%$)
4. **Unfamiliar with Paytm Features:** $17$ mentions ($15.32\%$)
5. **No Compelling Reason to Switch:** $17$ mentions ($15.32\%$)
6. **Another App Has Better Rewards:** $14$ mentions ($12.61\%$)
7. **Trust Another App More:** $14$ mentions ($12.61\%$)
8. **"Nothing — I Could Use Paytm More":** $14$ mentions ($12.61\%$)
